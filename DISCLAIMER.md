# Disclaimer

This file is the exact disclaimer text shown in the UI (`app.py`, sourced verbatim from
`config.DISCLAIMER`) and in the CLI output. It matches PRD Appendix A.

> **Facts only. No investment advice.** Answers are generated from HDFC public pages listed
> in the source list and may be out of date. Mutual fund investments are subject to market
> risks; read the scheme's SID/KIM and factsheet before investing. Please do not share PAN,
> Aadhaar, account numbers, OTPs, or any personal information.

The string rendered on screen, character-for-character, is:

```
Facts only. No investment advice. Answers are generated from HDFC public pages listed in the source list and may be out of date. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read the scheme's SID/KIM and factsheet before investing. Please do not share PAN, Aadhaar, account numbers, OTPs, or any personal information.
```

Every answer additionally carries the one-line footer `Facts only. Not investment advice.`
